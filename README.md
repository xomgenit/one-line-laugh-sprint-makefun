Entry for the [One-Line Laugh Sprint](https://make.fun/c/u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-laugh-sprint-cda708b2) bounty on make.fun — one original line, at most 200 characters, a light funny take on everyday life.

## The line

> I bought a standing desk to be healthier, so now I sit on a taller chair.

75 characters. The twist pays off on the last word: the desk meant to get me standing just got me a taller chair.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (via `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when the harness sets it, `out/` otherwise). Nothing else — one line, one file.
